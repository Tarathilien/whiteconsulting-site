Privacy

How I handle your information.

Short version: I collect only what I need to help you, I use it only for that, and I never sell or share it.

What I collect

Your name, email address and organization name, if you give them. Your answers to the assessment. Anything you write to me in a message or booking request.

Why

To send you your report, follow up about it, schedule calls, and understand where I can help. I do not use your information for anything else.

Who sees it

Only me. I never sell or share your information, and I do not send it to advertisers.

The AI service

To write the custom parts of your report, your answers are processed by an AI service. Your name, email address and organization name are not sent to it.

Where it is stored

Assessment responses and contact details are stored in a private Google Sheet and Google Apps Script account that only I can open. This website is hosted on Cloudflare. Booking runs through Google Calendar.

How long I keep it

I keep it until you ask me to delete it. This lets me follow up and pick up where we left off if you come back later.

Delete or correct your information

Email jared@whiteconsulting.org and ask. I will remove your information and confirm when it is done.

Changes

If I change how I handle information, I will update this page and the date below.
