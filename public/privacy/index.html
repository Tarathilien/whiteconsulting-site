Privacy

How I handle your information.

Short version: I collect only what I need to send your report and follow up, I never sell it, and I delete it after 12 months unless you become a client.

What I collect

When you take the free assessment:

  • Your name, email address and organization name.
  • Basic facts about your organization: its type, its size range, who looks after its computers and accounts, your main priority, and which kinds of sensitive information it handles, such as health information or card payments. I ask only which kinds you handle, never for the information itself.
  • Your answers, including which AI tools your staff use, and your results.
  • How you reached the assessment (the website, a business card tap, an outreach link or in person), and when you gave consent and the wording you agreed to.

If you book a meeting, I also have what you enter on the booking form and the meeting itself (see Meetings below). If you write to me, I have what you send.

The assessment has no free-text boxes. Please do not enter health details about specific people, client records or passwords on this site or in the booking form.

Why

To send your report, follow up about it, schedule and prepare for meetings, and, if you become a client, do the work you hired me for. I do not use your information for anything else.

Emails you will get

The consent box on the assessment covers your report (an email with a PDF copy) and up to two short check-in emails, about 7 and 30 days later, if you have not booked a meeting. After that I stop unless you contact me. Clients also get emails about their work.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also reply and ask. Either way, I stop within 10 business days, usually much sooner.

The AI service

The wording of your report is written with help from an AI service, Claude, made by Anthropic. It receives only your answers, your organization type and size range, your main priority and the AI tools you listed. It never receives your name, email address or organization name. Your score and your gaps are calculated by my own code; the AI only puts them into plain words.

Anthropic does not use this information to train its AI models, and deletes it within 30 days unless it must keep it longer for safety or legal reasons.

Meetings

Meetings run on Google Meet and are recorded and transcribed by default, so I can focus on you instead of taking notes. You will see a notice when you book and get an email about it. To opt out, reply “no recording” to that email or tell me at the start of the call, and I will not record. I confirm at the start of every call either way.

Transcription is handled by Wispr Flow. I may use an AI assistant, Claude, to summarize my notes, on an account set so that content is not used to train AI models. Please do not share health information about specific people during meetings.

Who sees it

Only me. I never sell your information or give it to anyone for marketing. The services below store or process it for me so I can do the work.

Where it is stored

  • Google: assessment records in a private Google Sheet that only I can open, plus email (Gmail), bookings (Google Calendar) and meetings (Google Meet).
  • Anthropic: the AI wording of your report, as described above.
  • Wispr Flow: meeting recordings and transcripts.
  • Cloudflare: hosts this website. Its fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive standard connection details such as your IP address.

This site does not use advertising trackers or cookies to follow you. While you take the assessment, your progress stays in your browser tab only. If you leave before finishing, nothing is saved.

I keep anonymous counts of how many people start and finish the assessment, which questions are reached, and how visitors arrived. These counts contain no names, email addresses or other details that identify you.

How long I keep it

  • If you do not become a client: each assessment and everything tied to it is deleted 12 months after the date you took it. Booking a meeting does not change this; only a signed agreement does. This includes copies in my email, calendar and meeting notes.
  • If you become a client: I keep your records while we work together, and afterward for as long as I need them for business, tax and legal records.
  • At any time: ask me to delete your information, and I will within 30 days.

If you take the assessment again, each one is kept under the same record so you can see your progress.

Delete, correct or unsubscribe

Email jared@whiteconsulting.org and ask. I will delete or correct your information within 30 days, including copies in my email, calendar and meeting notes, and confirm when it is done. Copies held by the AI service are deleted on its own schedule, described above.
