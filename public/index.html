Myth or fact

What most small organizations get wrong about AI.

Myth

“We don’t officially use AI, so we don’t need rules.”

Fact

78% of workers who use AI at work bring their own tools, according to Microsoft and LinkedIn’s 2024 Work Trend Index. Only 39% had received AI training from their employer.

Myth

“Policies are for big companies.”

Fact

In a December 2025 survey of 346 nonprofits, 92% were using AI and 47% had no AI policy at all (Virtuous, 2026 Nonprofit AI Adoption Report).

Myth

“Our staff would never paste client information into a chatbot.”

Fact

One 2026 analysis of workplace AI use found that about 40% of AI interactions involved sensitive data, and nearly a third of ChatGPT use was on personal accounts (Cyberhaven).
