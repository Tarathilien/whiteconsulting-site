Real work for local nonprofits

Here is what I actually do.

Two organizations with no IT department. Two problems fixed.

Habitat for Humanity of Greater Jonesboro

Email that works again.

What I did: Resolved Google Workspace issues, corrected domain (DNS) configuration at GoDaddy, and restored reliable email sending and receiving.

The result: Email had been down for 6 weeks while the organization was waiting on grant funding news. I restored it in 5 days, and grant notifications and funding communications were flowing again.

Agape House of Paragould

Locked out since March. Back in control in two weeks.

What I did: Their two existing admins could not fix it. I opened a support case with Google, answered its verification questions, and got a staff member approved as the Google Workspace admin. I also recovered the domain registration and found 8 existing accounts no one could access.

The result: Workspace access, lost since March 2026, was restored in 14 days (November 7 to 21). They can now onboard new staff and remove departing staff, with secure credential sharing.
